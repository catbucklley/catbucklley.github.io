Favorite Characters

*Spoiler Warning*



Darrow from Pierce Brown's Red Rising

Darrow is one of my favorite characters because of how determined he is, but he still remains human. At the beginning of the story Darrow is filled with questions and uncertainty, but he eventually embraces the position he is in.

Elspeth Spindle from Rachel Gillig's One Dark Window

Elspeth is one of my all-time favorite book characters. As compared to other female main character's in fantasy stories, Elspeth is extremly reserved and kind-hearted.

Aelin Galathnius from Sarah J. Maas' Throne of Glass Series

Aelin, while the complete opposite of Elspeth, is extremly independent and self-sufficient.
